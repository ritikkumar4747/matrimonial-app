## 🌟 Guru's Key Principles

### The Healing Timeline
1. **Days 1-3:** Allow the pain (cry, feel, be present)
2. **Days 4-14:** Gentle self-care (move, nourish, connect)
3. **Weeks 3-6:** Intentional healing (understand, journal, grow)
4. **Week 7+:** Integration (accept, rebuild, open again)

### What Makes a Soul Mate
✨ Someone who CHOOSES you every day  
✨ Someone willing to grow with you  
✨ Someone who challenges you to be better  
✨ Someone who loves your flaws  
✨ Someone who makes you want to be your best self  

### Red Flags (Never Ignore)
🚩 ANY form of abuse  
🚩 Dishonesty & lying  
🚩 Controlling behavior  
🚩 Isolation from loved ones  
🚩 Disrespecting boundaries  
🚩 Love bombing (too fast)  

### Green Flags (Strong Signs)
✅ Respects your independence  
✅ Makes time consistently  
✅ Shows interest in YOUR life  
✅ Supports your dreams  
✅ Introduces you to family  
✅ Takes responsibility  
✅ Listens without judging  
✅ Shows affection naturally

## 💪 When to Never Give Up

**DO PERSIST IF:**
- ✅ They treat you with respect
- ✅ You share core values
- ✅ You both are willing to grow
- ✅ Love is mutual
- ✅ You feel genuinely happy
- ✅ You inspire each other
- ✅ Your gut says STAY

**DO GIVE UP IF:**
- ❌ They don't respect you
- ❌ They're abusive in any way
- ❌ Only one person is trying
- ❌ They're unwilling to grow
- ❌ Your peace is sacrificed
- ❌ Your instincts scream RUN
